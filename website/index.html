Plumbing & Drain FAQ

Quick answers to what homeowners ask us most.

What's the difference between drain snaking and jetting?

A snake punches through a clog so water can flow again. Jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the full inside of the pipe, washing away grease, sludge and roots. For repeat clogs, jetting usually lasts much longer.

How do I know if it's a main sewer line problem?

If more than one fixture backs up at once, like the toilet gurgles when the washer drains or water comes up through the basement floor drain, the problem is usually the main line. A video pipe inspection confirms the cause.

Do I need to replace my sewer line?

Not always. Many problems clear with jetting. If the camera shows a collapsed, broken or badly root-damaged pipe, replacement is often the permanent fix. We'll show you the footage and explain your options.

What is a sewage ejector pump?

If you have a bathroom or laundry in the basement below your sewer line, an ejector pump lifts the waste up so it can drain away. We install new systems and repair existing ones.

Do you give the price before starting?

Yes. We explain what we found and what it will cost before any work begins.

Do you work with businesses too?

Yes. We handle residential and commercial plumbing, including backflow preventers, meter pits and new underground plumbing.
